# /sponsor

Mode: Persuade. A company decides to sponsor and leaves its details.

- Audience: AI and dev-tool companies (want developers and founders on their product), companies hiring and talent agencies (want to meet engineers and founders). Investors second.
- Action: the form (name, email, company); it sends the organizer a message. No prices, no package list (user, 2026-11-18).
- Proof: the community's numbers from Luma registrations (sample values until supplied), a real meetup photo. The sponsor deck's "Who's in the room" slide is the agreed base for the numbers; average attendance only, no registrations.
- Constraints: name Lisbon; site copy rules; brand parts only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The community is shown before anything is asked: pitch and form share the first viewport over a packed Lisbon meetup, and the numbers make the community countable, one square per person. Refuses the category default: tier cards, a logo wall, a generic split-screen SaaS form.

OWN-WORLD: agnteng.com as it stands. Electric-blue hero block; a meetup photo in the blue tone breaking up into blue cells; a deep-navy section with the live brand dither, as on the sponsor deck; Manrope extra-bold headings, Kode Mono small uppercase labels; a white form card with the brand's 10px fields and pill buttons.

STORY: A company learns this is a monthly Lisbon meetup of founders and developers building with AI, sees who comes and what they are looking for, believes it reaches the people it wants, and leaves its details.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: nav on blue; H1 (the user's hook) and one sponsor line in the left half; the white form card in the right five columns, its lower edge over a full-bleed blue-toned crowd band whose top dissolves into the blue as cells. Phone: H1, line, card, band.

FORM: Code-led, no image generation. A precisely specified surface inside the established world (pitch left, form right; the deck slide as the numbers' base), so no concept roll and no seed key. Signature interaction: the community bar, one square per person, its colours sweeping in column by column once it scrolls into view. Motion: the hero's rise-in, the live cells and dither, that one sweep.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
