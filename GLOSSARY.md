# Agentic Engineering

The meetup, its community, and the companies that back it.

## Language

**Partner**:
A company that backs one or more meetups and gets the Partner package in return.
_Avoid_: sponsor (for the company; "sponsor" is only the verb and the agnteng.com page)

**Proposal**:
The private page written for one company we want as a Partner, with what the partnership includes and its price.
_Avoid_: deck, pitch
